# ML AI Papers

A curated repository of research papers in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence. This collection aims to provide easy access to seminal works, recent breakthroughs, and comprehensive surveys in the field of ML/AI.

---

## Overview

This repository is designed for students, researchers, and enthusiasts looking to deepen their understanding of modern AI and machine learning concepts. It includes:

- **Seminal Papers:** Foundational works that have shaped the field.
- **Recent Breakthroughs:** Cutting-edge research and innovations.
- **Surveys and Reviews:** Comprehensive summaries and surveys for quick insights.
- **Educational Resources:** Supplementary materials such as video lectures and articles to aid your learning journey.

---


- **`seminal_papers/`**: Contains classic papers that have established the basis for modern ML and AI.
- **`recent_papers/`**: Holds the latest research articles and breakthrough studies.
- **`surveys/`**: Includes review articles and surveys that summarize the state-of-the-art in various domains.
- **`resources/`**: Additional links, video lectures, and reading lists for extended learning.

---

## How to Use

- **Browse:** Navigate through the folders to find papers by topic or publication date.
- **Learn:** Use the included summaries and external resources to get context before diving into the full texts.
- **Contribute:** If you find a paper that is not included or have additional resources, feel free to submit a pull request.
